ON "CONFIDENCE"

Question:

How can I be more confident?"

Answer:

Faith doesn't stay inside you like a baby. Confidence is a sun you look up at the sunrise. Walk into a room and feel like you have your life in your pocket and your hands on the wheel. Roll your shoulders back. When someone asks what you're doing, say, "I'm working." It explains everything and nothing at the same time.

And besides, you can't always listen to your intellect. The brain is great at doing calculations, checking the temperature of your soup, and remembering where you left your keys. You have to take on the role of mayor of your brain and "build a house".

Remember: you don’t have to be a certified dog. Lions have their problems too. You just have to be someone who doesn’t ask permission from everyone you meet on the street.
